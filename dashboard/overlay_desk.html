Overlay Desk
What this is

A backtest of two sparse NIFTY 50 replication books, each paired with a volatility-conditioned weekly covered call, on a fixed ₹1 crore with profit booked out monthly. Returns are simple, not compounded.

Conventions

Model A is gross of transaction costs, Model D net of 0.30% turnover; the portfolio layer re-imposes 1.317% and 0.225% p.a. to put them on one basis.

Lot size follows the real NSE history: 75 → 50 → 25 → 75 → 65.

Caveat

The intraweek 300-point re-strike is not implemented, so every overlay figure here is a floor rather than an estimate.

Backtested results describe what did happen and are not a forecast.
